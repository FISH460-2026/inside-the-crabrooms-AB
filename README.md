## How do different crab traps affect the stress of native shore crabs?
Deryn Bagley, Sophie Monterola, Hannah Tucker

## Materials List:
- 1 Carcinus maenas (European Green Crab)
- 4 Hemigrapsus oregonensis (Hairy Shore Crab), 8 Hemigrapsus nudus
- 4 osmometer(?) chambers
- Fabric mesh for “Fukui” trap
- Mud/silt 
- Foil / blacked out material for “crab slab”
- Rock / fake crab

## Methods
### Week 1
1) label
2) weigh
3) righting time
4) resazurin
5) put in makeshift traps

### Other Weeks 
1) Take out of traps 
2) Righting time
3) Resazurin
4) Put back into traps
